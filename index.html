Frequently asked questions

How long does a Botox visit take?

Most visits are short once the talk is done. The talk itself takes the most time, because the team wants to hear your goals and map your face. The injections take only a few minutes, and many people go back to work or errands the same day.

When will I see a change?

Some change can show in a few days, and the full softening is often clear in about two weeks. That timing can shift a bit from person to person, so the team will tell you what to watch for and when to check in.

Will my face look frozen?

It should not, when the dose and placement fit your face. The goal at K-Glow MD Aesthetics is a rested look with movement still there. Tell the team if you want a very light touch, and they can plan for that.

Does it hurt?

Most people say it feels like a tiny pinch that passes fast. The needles are very small, and the visit is quick. If you are nervous, say so at the start. The team can slow down and talk you through each step.

How long do results last?

For many people, results last about three to four months. Movement, dose, and your own body all play a part. A follow-up visit can help you keep the look you like without a big gap in between.

Can I pair Botox with fillers or a laser?

Yes, many people do. Botox eases lines from movement, fillers add volume, and lasers help tone and texture. The team can build a plan that spaces visits in a way that fits your skin and your week.

What should I do after my visit?
